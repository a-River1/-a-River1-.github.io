Projects

Characterization of microRNA in Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease

Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD) is described as a chronic acid reflux condition. GERD and subsequent complications is characterized by certain inflammatory proteins. MicroRNAs can regulate the mRNAs responsible for said proteins. This research study aimed to better understand the role that microRNA plays in GERD.

Synthesis of PPP1b9r Circular RNA and Assessment of its Structure

Formation of primers for circRNA transcripts to better understand how G-quadruplex structures form off of RNA fragments. Linear counterparts of the PPP1b9r fragment form G-quadruplex structures which often lead to the formation of lewi bodies and eventually Parkinson's. Forcing circular RNA fragments to take their palce could alter this.
